Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code? +

The dress code is Formal. We'd love for you to wear beige or soft neutral tones to complement our wedding palette.

💛 Gentle Reminder

Please avoid wearing black, white, or pink attire.

Is there parking available? +

Yes, parking is available near the venue. Parking attendants will be on-site to assist you at both the Kingdom Hall and the reception. You may park with ease and join us for the celebration.

Can we take photos during the ceremony? +

Yes, we'd love for you to capture the memories! At the ceremony, please be discreet and mindful in taking pictures. We would appreciate it if you take them at your seats. Also, please avoid using flash. At the reception, feel free to take as many photos as you'd like! Please use our wedding hashtag: #YouJayvMeSamOneToLove

What about gifts? +

Your love and presence mean everything to us—that is truly all we ask. But if your heart wishes to bless us further, monetary gifts would be lovingly appreciated as we build our new life together.

Where should I sit at the reception? +

Our ushers and usherettes will warmly welcome you and guide you to your reserved seat.

What happens at a Wedding of Jehovah's Witnesses? +

A Jehovah's Witness wedding is a beautiful, heartfelt ceremony centered on Bible principles and the blessing of marriage. To learn more about what to expect, visit JW.org
